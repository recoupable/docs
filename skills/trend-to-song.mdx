---
title: "Turn a trend into a song"
description: "Reverse the traditional workflow. Start from a cultural moment and build a song + distribution plan around it."
---

Triggers when someone identifies a trending topic, viral moment, reality TV drama, meme, or cultural event and wants to turn it into a song with a campaign. Phrases like "make a song about this trend," "capitalize on this moment," "trend-jack," or "burner project."

## Install

```bash
npx skills add recoupable/skills --skill trend-to-song
```
